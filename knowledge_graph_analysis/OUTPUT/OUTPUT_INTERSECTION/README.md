## This folder Contains multiple subfolders and files:

 1_Diabetes_Intersection: Contains the overlapping targets between SHD-modulated proteins and type 2 diabetes-associated targets. <br>

 2_Obesity_Intersection: Contains the overlapping targets between SHD-modulated proteins and obesity-associated targets. <br>

 3_Shared_Comorbidity_Intersection: Contains the overlapping targets between SHD-modulated proteins and targets associated with both Type 2 Diabetes and obesity. <br>

 KG_Diabetes_Total_HighConfidence.csv: Contains targets associated with type 2 diabetes that are modulated by at least three SHDs. <br>

 KG_Obesity_Total_HighConfidence.csv: Contains targets associated with obesity that are modulated by at least three SHDs. <br>
 
 KG_Shared_Total_HighConfidence.csv: Contains targets associated with both type 2 diabetes and obesity that are modulated by at least three SHDs.
